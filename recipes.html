Recipes

Simple Recipes

Vanilla Cake

Mix flour, sugar, baking powder, eggs, milk, butter, and vanilla. Pour the batter into a prepared cake pan. Bake at 350°F for approximately 30 minutes.

Chocolate Chip Cookies

Mix butter, sugar, eggs, vanilla, flour, baking soda, and chocolate chips. Place spoonfuls of dough onto a baking sheet and bake at 350°F for 10–12 minutes.

Vanilla Cupcakes

Prepare a simple vanilla cake batter and divide it between cupcake liners. Bake at 350°F for 18–20 minutes, then cool before decorating.

Cake Pops

Crumble cooled cake and mix it with a small amount of frosting. Roll it into balls, add sticks, chill, and cover each cake pop with melted chocolate.
